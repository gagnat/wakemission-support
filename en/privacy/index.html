Privacy

Your data stays yours.

WakeMission is designed to do its work on your iPhone while collecting as little personal information as possible.

The short version

Alarm data and reference photos are stored locally. WakeMission version 1 has no user account, cloud storage, analytics, advertising SDK, or tracking.

Last updated 3 September 2026

1. Data controller

WakeMission is provided by Øystein Gagnat in Norway. Privacy questions may be sent to gagnat.apps@gmail.com.

2. Information processed in the app

Alarms and settings

Times, repeat schedules, selected missions, alarm history, points, progress, and preferences are stored locally so the features you choose can work.

Reference photos

Photos you create for image missions are stored and compared on your device. They are not uploaded to us.

Camera, motion, and alarm access

The camera is used only when you choose a camera or image mission. Motion data is used to determine whether a movement mission is complete. Alarm and notification access is used to schedule and present alarms you create. You control these permissions in iOS.

Purchases

Apple processes payments and payment information. The app receives only the entitlement information needed to unlock content. We do not receive your payment-card details.

3. When you contact support

When you email us, we process information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, attachments, and any device, iOS, or app-version details. It is used only to reply, troubleshoot, and follow up on your case.

Support correspondence is handled through Google's Gmail service and may therefore be processed by Google as a service provider under its terms and applicable transfer safeguards.

4. Legal basis and sharing

On-device processing is used to provide features you request. Purchase status is processed to fulfil your purchase. Support information is processed to answer your request and for our legitimate interest in secure, effective support.

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed for purchases or support, or when required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Local app data remains until you remove it in the app or delete the app. Copies in a device backup are controlled by Apple's backup settings.
  • Reference photos can be removed in WakeMission's photo library.
  • Support correspondence is kept as long as needed to resolve and document the case, then deleted or anonymised when no longer needed.

6. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to processing of personal information received through support. Contact us by email. You may also lodge a complaint with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.

7. Changes

This notice is updated when features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
